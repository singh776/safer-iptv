About SaFar

SaFar IPTV Player ("SaFar", "the app") is a video player for Android TV, phones and tablets, available on Google Play. It does not provide or include any channels, films, series or subscriptions. You sign in with a playlist or account from an IPTV service you already use.

This policy explains what the app stores, what leaves your device, and the choices you have. In short: we do not collect your personal information. There is no server of ours that receives anything about you.

Uninstalling SaFar, or clearing its data in Android Settings → Apps, deletes all of this. Inside the app you can also remove an account and clear your history at any time.

What leaves your device

SaFar sends data only to make the app work, and never to the developer.

  • Your IPTV service. The login you enter is sent to the server you entered, and nowhere else. That service provides your lists, details and videos, and its own privacy practices apply.
  • Pictures and trailers. To show posters and play trailers, the app asks the public online servers that your IPTV service or the film listing points to. Like any website, they can see your device's internet address and the picture or title requested. No login details are sent to them.
  • Your phone, on your own Wi‑Fi. If you choose "Sign in with your phone", the app opens a small page on your TV that is reachable only on your own home network and protected by a one‑time code. What you type goes straight to your TV, never to the internet. The page closes after sign‑in or after 5 minutes.

SaFar has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind, and does not sell or share personal information.

SaFar does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Updates

SaFar is installed and updated through Google Play. The app does not check for updates by itself and sends nothing about you for that purpose.

Your content, your responsibility

SaFar is only a player. The developer has no access to, and no control over, what your IPTV service provides. Please use SaFar only with content you have the right to watch.

Google Play data safety

  • Data collected by the developer: none.
  • Data shared: none for advertising or analytics. Your login is sent only to the IPTV server you enter.
  • Encrypted in transit: yes, whenever your server and the online services support a secure connection.
  • Deleting your data: uninstall the app or clear its data. Nothing is held anywhere else.

Children

SaFar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
