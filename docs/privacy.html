SaFar IPTV Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

What SaFar is

SaFar IPTV Player ("SaFar") is a video player for Android TV, phones and tablets. It does not provide any channels, films, series or subscriptions. You sign in with a playlist or account from an IPTV service you already use.

Information SaFar stores

The playlist links, server addresses, usernames, passwords and MAC addresses you enter, your settings, your watch history (Continue Watching) and cached lists of titles are stored only on your device.

Information SaFar sends

SaFar connects only to the servers you enter, to load your lists and play your videos, and to show film pictures and trailers those lists point to. SaFar has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising, and does not send your information to the developer or to anyone else.

Sign in with your phone (QR code)

When you choose to sign in with your phone, SaFar starts a small web page on your TV that is reachable only on your own WiFi network, protected by a one-time code. The details you type on your phone go straight to your TV — never to the internet or to the developer. The page closes after you sign in, when you leave the screen, or after 5 minutes.

Device information

SaFar may show your device's local network address and MAC address on screen (for example, for MAC portal sign-in). This information is not sent anywhere by SaFar.

Updates

The Google Play version is updated by Google Play. The version downloaded from our website checks our download page for a newer version; this check sends no personal information.

Deleting your information

Remove an account in Settings → IPTV account, clear Play History in Settings, or uninstall SaFar to delete everything it stored.

Children

SaFar is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
