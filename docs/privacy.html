Privacy Policy

Effective November 14, 2026

In short: Safar is only a player. It has no account system, no ads and no tracking. Everything you set up in Safar is saved on your own device, and we never collect, see or sell your information.

1Your data stays on your device

Your IPTV account, Favourites, Continue Watching, where you stopped in a film, and all your settings are saved only on the TV, phone or tablet you use. Safar has no server of its own that stores them.

2No account, no sign-up

Safar never asks for your name, email address or phone number, and you don't create an account with us.

3No ads, no tracking

There are no advertisements in Safar. What you watch, search or save is never recorded, analysed or shared — not with us and not with anyone else.

4Your passwords

Your IPTV username and password are kept only on your device and are sent only to your own IPTV server, to sign you in. We never receive them.

5Safar is only a player

Safar is a media player. It does not include, host, sell or provide any channels, films, series or playlists. Everything you watch plays from the IPTV service you add yourself.

  • Safar has no rights to, and no control over, that content, and is not connected with, endorsed by or responsible for any IPTV provider.
  • You are responsible for making sure you have the right to watch the content you add.
  • Any questions about channels, films or your IPTV subscription belong with your IPTV provider.

6What Safar connects to

Only what it needs to work: your own IPTV server (to show and play your lists), pictures for films and channels, and a check for a newer version of Safar. Nothing about you is sent with these requests.

7Sign in with your phone

Scanning the code on your TV works only inside your home WiFi. The details you type go straight from your phone to your TV — never over the internet — and the code stops working after 5 minutes.

8Payments

If you choose a plan, it is paid through the app store you installed Safar from. Safar never sees your card or bank details. The free trial and the plan can be cancelled any time in the store's Subscriptions.

9Permissions

Internet — to play your channels and films. Installing updates — only to put a newer Safar on your device when you press Update. Safar does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

10Children

Safar does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

11Delete your data

In Safar, use Clear Play History, remove your IPTV account, or simply uninstall the app — everything is deleted from your device. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, there is nothing more to delete.

12Security

Your information never leaves your device except to reach your own IPTV server. Keep your device protected with a screen lock if others use it.

13Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page and shown inside Safar, with a new effective date.
